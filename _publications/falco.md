This paper proposes FALCO, a novel framework that leverages foundation models to guide active learning for cost-effective off-road freespace detection. By utilizing the rich contextual understanding of foundation models like VLM, FALCO efficiently identifies informative samples for annotation, significantly reducing the labeling effort while maintaining high detection performance.
